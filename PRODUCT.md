# AI Lead Manager

## Register

product

## Users

Agencies and larger sales organizations managing business enquiries and opportunities. Sales staff review a shared pipeline at their desks and check upcoming conversations from mobile devices. The audience was confirmed by the user on 11 October 2026.

## Product Purpose

Make incoming leads easy to qualify, prioritize, and follow up. The existing product creates leads, receives AI scores and suggested responses, tracks four pipeline stages, and schedules follow-ups. Success means a salesperson can identify the next useful action and understand the supporting information quickly.

## Brand Personality

Warm, considered, professional. The user requested Airbnb-inspired styling, careful dashboard design informed by Apple and Google guidance, and reusable desktop and mobile interfaces. Familiar interactions and confident typography should make the workspace approachable without reducing information quality.

## Anti-references

Avoid the current long page of disconnected metric cards, heavy navy headers, repeated shadows, generic template assets, jargon without explanation, inaccessible hover-only help, decorative controls that do not work, and ambiguous failures presented as an empty pipeline.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next useful action close to the information that supports it.
2. Give overview, lead exploration, follow-ups, and insights distinct places in one coherent workspace.
3. Make AI recommendations explainable and distinguish sample data from connected production data.
4. Reuse an accessible component vocabulary and consistent interaction patterns.
5. Adapt the structure to small screens while retaining every core workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA practices: keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic labels, accessible names, contrast, textual status indicators, generous touch targets, reduced-motion support, and help available through hover, keyboard focus, and touch. Use readable defaults and responsive reflow without depending on color alone.
